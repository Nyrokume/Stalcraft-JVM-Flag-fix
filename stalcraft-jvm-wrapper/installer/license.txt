After installation:
1. Keep stalcraft-jvm-wrapper.exe and service.exe in the same folder.
2. Open the app and click INSTALL (UAC) to register IFEO hooks.
3. Click VERIFY — all 6 targets must be OK.
4. Fully restart the game launcher, then start the game.
5. Do not run service.exe manually.
